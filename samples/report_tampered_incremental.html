Limits

Limits of this check: if someone retypes the rate confirmation from scratch or prints it to a new PDF, metadata and font checks may find nothing. Comparing the driver's copy with the original the broker sent is the strongest test. Email headers can show the sending mail server's approximate location; a PDF itself usually does not store an IP address. This tool reports signs of editing. It does not prove fraud, and it is not a legal finding.
